About ScoringCo

Building trust in Kenya's digital economy

Who we are

The Scoring Company is Kenya's first decentralized trust verification registry. We connect verified business profiles with authentic customer feedback — helping buyers make informed decisions.

Our mission

To eliminate information asymmetry in local commerce by providing transparent, community-driven business intelligence.

Why trust matters

Every year, thousands of Kenyans lose money to unverified sellers. We give power back to consumers through verified badges, honest reviews, and a fraud reporting system.
